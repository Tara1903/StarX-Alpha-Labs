## 1. PROPOSAL OBJECTIVE & ASSUMPTIONS

This document outlines the commercial pricing proposal for all standardized StarX solutions. Every proposed price is backed by empirical engineering delivery models, software component reuse, and Google Stitch UI acceleration.

### Baseline Assumptions
1. **Accelerated Delivery:** StarX achieves 3x–4x faster time-to-market by leveraging proprietary architecture blueprints, Next.js 15 starter kits, and Google Stitch MCP generative UI tooling.
2. **Standard Senior Engineering Rate:** Calculated at ₹1,800/hr ($22/hr) for Indian domestic delivery; $75/hr for international client delivery.
3. **Blended Gross Margin Target:** Minimum 48% target gross margin on all fixed development tiers; 65% gross margin on monthly managed operations retainers.
4. **Third-Party Pass-Through:** Gemini API, OpenAI tokens, ElevenLabs voice minutes, and SMS/WhatsApp charges are separated and billed at transparent usage rates to protect agency margins.
